#+TITLE: Puzzleten research

This directory holds the outcomes of research the user explicitly asked for. It
informs the specs in [[file:../design/index.org][design/]]; it is not itself a design.
A research document explains what was found, offers recommendations and scenarios to
choose from, and leaves the decision to the user. The decision then goes into a spec
or an ADR.

* When to write one
Whenever the user explicitly asks to research or investigate something, the outcome
is written here as an Org document, in addition to being reported in the conversation.
Don't create one for questions that are quickly answered in conversation or for
investigations done as a step of other work.

* Conventions
- File name: =short-title.org=, with the topic as title (e.g. =disabling-contracts.org=).
- Properties drawer at the top with =DATE= (today) and =STATUS=:
  =draft= (still being written), =final= (complete, awaiting or informing a decision),
  or =superseded= (with =SUPERSEDED-BY=).
- Sections, skipping the ones that don't apply:
  1. *Question*: what was asked and why, in one or two sentences.
  2. *Summary*: the answer and the recommendation, short enough to read alone.
  3. *Findings*: what was found, with evidence (measurements, source and documentation
     references, how an experiment was run), so it can be checked or repeated.
  4. *Scenarios*: the realistic options to choose from, each with what it takes, what
     it gains, what it costs or risks, and its measured effect where there is one.
  5. *Recommendation*: which scenario to take and under which conditions another is better.
  6. *Open questions*: what the user must decide or what could not be established.
  7. *Related*: links to specs, decisions, and other research.
- Separate what was measured or read from what is inferred, and say how reliable the
  numbers are (machine, repetitions, noise).
- Don't change the code base as part of research. Experiments run on scratch copies;
  report anything that needs a change as a recommendation.
- Supporting files (scripts, raw output) go in =data/<title>/=, and the document says how to repeat the measurements.
- Link to the research from the spec it informs, once there is one.
